# LinguaStories Feature List

LinguaStories is a focused, self-guided language-learning application.

## Public experience

- Learning-focused landing page
- Account registration and login
- Traditional Chinese course from English

## Course delivery

- Versioned curriculum manifest and category packages
- Download progress, checksum verification, and schema validation
- IndexedDB installation with atomic activation
- Offline lesson access and content-health repair
- Safe content updates and prior-version retention

## Learning path

- Sequential category and lesson unlocking
- Short lessons combining vocabulary, dialogue, sentences, and stories
- Multiple-choice, matching, ordering, listening, typing, and reading interactions
- Saved lesson attempts and resumable sessions
- Perfect-pass completion rules

## Review and progress

- Spaced-repetition scheduling
- Due, weak-item, and optional practice queues
- Learning streaks, study time, completion, and accuracy insights
- Category progress and completed-story reader

## Settings and privacy

- Pinyin, translation, story display, audio, and review preferences
- Reduced-motion support
- Course update and offline-storage controls
- Local progress reset and application-data reset
- Curriculum and metric definitions

## Platform

- PostgreSQL-backed account authentication and sessions
- Responsive desktop and mobile learning layouts
- Versioned generated curriculum and validation tooling
